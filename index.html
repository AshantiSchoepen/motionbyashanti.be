WHO

ANYWAY?

I am a final-year Motion Graphics student at AP Hogeschool Antwerpen, with a focus on motion design and visual storytelling. My work mainly explores animation, graphic design, and 3D visuals, combining clean composition with experimentation in form and movement.During my studies, I completed an internship at DB Video, where I gained hands-on experience in a professional production environment and further developed my skills in video editing, motion design, and post-production workflows.

In addition to my internship, I have worked on a range of school projects and personal experiments, building experience in Adobe After Effects, Premiere Pro, Illustrator, and Photoshop, as well as basic programming and web-related projects. I enjoy working across different visual disciplines, from animated content to graphic layouts and 3D-based visuals, and I’m always looking to improve both the technical and creative sides of my work.

I am currently looking for a student job until the end of September. From October onwards, I will be available for a full-time position in motion design or related creative roles.
