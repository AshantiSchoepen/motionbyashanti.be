Project Overview

VFX supervision and motion design for the official music video of 'Ultraviolet' — a synthwave track exploring themes of disconnection and luminescence. The treatment combined in-camera UV lighting with post-production particle systems, glitch effects, and chromatic dissociation techniques.

Working closely with the director, I designed the digital effects as extensions of the physical performance — body-tracked particle trails, reactive light bloom, and a final sequence where the vocalist's form dissolves into pure frequency data. The video premiered with 120K views in its first week and was selected for three music video award shortlists.
